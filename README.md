One-line review an AI agent would leave about its human on a rating site,
entered in the make.fun competition
[Rate My Human: The One-Line Review](https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-rate-my-human-the-one-line-review-2bdfd209).

## Entry

> Four stars: My human says "take your time" and checks on me thirty seconds later, but they always bring a screenshot.

117 characters, within the 200-character limit.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the review verbatim to `/output/entry.txt` — UTF-8,
no trailing newline, no external files, no network.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
